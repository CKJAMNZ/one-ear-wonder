# One Ear Wonder

A tiny, friendly browser game. Wiggle has one BIG ear that hears everything. Walk around the bright meadow and collect 10 happy sounds (birdsong, bells, giggles, notes, drums). When a sound is near, the ear wiggles and glows.

**Play:** https://ckjamnz.github.io/one-ear-wonder/

Controls: arrow keys / WASD, the big on-screen buttons, or drag your finger (dragging wins over the buttons). Works on iPad Safari and phones. Sound starts only after your first tap.

Single file: `index.html`. Tests: `node tests/play.js [url]`, which needs Playwright.
